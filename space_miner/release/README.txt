HOW TO RUN
----------
  Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or newer (it only uses components that
  ship with the operating system - no runtimes, no install, no .NET).
  Double-click SPACE_MINER.exe.
  (The installer SPACE_MINER_SETUP.exe only copies the game and makes
   shortcuts - the executable itself is completely self-contained.)
  If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning for an unsigned build, choose
  "More info" -> "Run anyway".

CONTROLS
--------
  W A S D ............ thrust             MOUSE ............... aim
  LEFT MOUSE ......... mining beam        RIGHT MOUSE ......... pulse cannons
  SHIFT .............. afterburner        SPACE ............... mining beam (alt)
  E .................. dock / undock at Helios Station
  TAB ................ cargo manifest and station interface
  M .................. sector chart / jump drive
  ESC ................ settings and pause menu
  H .................. toggle the FPS counter
  F11 ................ borderless fullscreen

THE LOOP
--------
  1. Fly out and hold the left mouse button near an asteroid.
     The beam eats the rock; the ore drops out as loot and is tractored
     into your hold once you are close enough.
  2. Fly back to Helios Station (the ring at the centre of the sector)
     and press E, then sell the cargo on the CARGO tab.
  3. Spend the credits on the twelve upgrade branches or a new hull.
  4. Unlock the next sector from the sector chart (M) and jump there.
  5. Buy mining drones - they fly out on their own, cut rock and bring
     the ore back to you.
  6. Hostile drones get in the way. Shoot back, or fly fast.

SECTORS
-------
  1 STARTER BELT     common ore, gentle                     (free)
  2 CRYSTAL NEBULA   crystals, ions, raiders               (2 500 CR)
  3 IRON GRAVEYARD   titanium, gold, heavy drones           (12 000 CR)
  4 PLASMA STORM     plasma ore, uranium, storm hazards      (45 000 CR)
  5 VOID FRONTIER    void crystal, quantum cores, sentinels (150 000 CR)

SAVING
------
  Progress is stored in  %APPDATA%\SpaceMiner\space_miner_save.dat
  with a checksum and a rolling backup (space_miner_save.bak).
  The game autosaves when you dock and every 90 seconds of flight.
  A damaged save is recovered instead of being deleted.
