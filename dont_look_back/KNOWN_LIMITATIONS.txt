DON'T LOOK BACK - known limitations
===================================

RENDERING
- The game uses a custom software renderer (the NIGHTFALL engine). It does not use
  your GPU. Frame rate therefore depends on CPU cores and clock speed: the frame is
  split into horizontal bands across all available cores (up to 8). A 4-core desktop
  CPU runs the default settings at roughly 30-45 fps at 1280x720.
- If the frame rate is low, open SETTINGS and reduce RENDER SCALE (80% is default,
  50-60% is a large speed-up with a soft look that suits the fog).
- Lighting is limited to 4 point lights plus the flashlight per pixel for speed;
  distant lamps in a long corridor do not contribute to the near surfaces.
- Volumetric light shafts are rendered at quarter resolution and blurred, which can
  show very slight softness around bright edges.

CONTENT / SYSTEMS
- The building has one playable floor (the third), plus the stairwell landings, the
  lift, the maintenance wing and the basement. The "fourth floor" is story, not
  geometry: it is deliberately unreachable.
- Sound is fully procedural (synthesised at start-up, no sampled assets). There is no
  voice acting; story is delivered through documents, notes and environmental cues.
- Anomalies are drawn from a pool of 30 and are not repeated within one playthrough;
  because there are exactly 30 and 12 are needed to finish, a session can see at most
  12 of them.
- There is one save slot (profile.sav in %APPDATA%\DontLookBack). Saving happens from
  the pause menu; there is no autosave or checkpoint.
- The installer installs per-user into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\DontLookBack and does
  not require administrator rights. There is no uninstaller entry; delete the folder
  and the two shortcuts to remove the game.
- The executable itself carries no embedded Win32 icon resource (no resource compiler
  was available in the build environment). The window icon, taskbar icon and the
  desktop/Start-menu shortcut icons are the real game icon (assets\ui\icon.ico),
  applied at start-up.
- Text is rendered with pre-baked bitmap fonts; at very large window sizes text is
  scaled up and becomes slightly soft.

TESTING
- All automated testing is headless (see TEST_RESULTS.txt): 114 checks covering
  world integrity, navigation, movement and collision, doors, flashlight, audio,
  all 30 anomalies (each is verified to actually change world state, sound or spawn
  an entity, and to reset cleanly), entity behaviour, reporting and progression,
  save/load, all three endings and the rendering path of six rooms.
- Behaviour that needs a human eye (framing, timing of scares, difficulty balance)
  was checked through rendered frames, not through automated tests.
