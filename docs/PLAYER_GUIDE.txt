SPACE MINER 1.0.0 — DEEP BELT OPERATIONS
========================================

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. No internet connection or additional runtime is required.

STARTING
--------
Run SPACE_MINER.exe. Select LAUNCH FLIGHT (or CONTINUE FLIGHT).
Your profile is saved locally and automatically during flight.

CONTROLS
--------
W A S D / arrow keys   Thrust and strafe
Mouse                  Aim ship and mining emitter
Left mouse (hold)      Mine the asteroid under the scanner reticle
Space / right mouse    Fire pulse cannons
Shift                  Afterburner (uses reactor energy)
E                      Dock / undock inside the Ceres Relay approach ring
Tab                    Expand or close the cargo manifest
M                      Open or close the sector chart
Esc                    Pause / close the current panel
F11                    Toggle fullscreen

YOUR FIRST MINING RUN
---------------------
1. Leave the station approach and find an asteroid.
2. Hover over it to scan its mineral, health, hardness and remaining yield.
3. Hold left mouse to mine. Keep the reactor above zero.
4. Fly near released mineral fragments to tractor them into your hold.
5. Return to the station, press E, and sell the hold in TRADE & REPAIR.
6. Install upgrades. A Drone Bay level unlocks your first autonomous drone slot.
7. Meet level and credit requirements in SECTOR CHART to unlock new regions.

SURVIVAL
--------
Shields begin recovering after four seconds without damage. Pulse cannons can
be fired while moving. If your hull fails, Ceres insurance redeploys the ship
and retains 75% of cargo. Watch for hostile contacts, storm target circles,
fast debris and void gravity wells.

SAVE DATA
---------
The versioned profile is stored in the Godot application user-data directory:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\SPACE MINER\space_miner_profile.json
A backup and a quarantined corrupt file may appear beside it. Do not edit the
checksum unless you are deliberately developing a save migration.

UNINSTALL
---------
Use Settings > Apps > SPACE MINER > Uninstall, or run Uninstall.exe in the
installation folder. Uninstalling does not delete your flight profile.
